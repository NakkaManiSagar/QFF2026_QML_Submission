The measured fidelity changed by -0.119873; this is the observed ideal-versus-noisy difference for these shots, not a claim beyond this measurement. The organizer model includes two-qubit depolarizing error on CX, single-qubit depolarizing error on the basis gates, and readout bit-flip error on measurement. These sources all contribute to the noisy result; this benchmark does not separately isolate their individual contributions.
